SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION NOTES
===========================================================================================================================================================
1. Experiment 016E does not run the model or modify any weights.
2. It scores only the responses already generated by Experiment 016D.
3. Answer correctness and format compliance are independent metrics.
4. CORRECT means a reliable final answer was present and matched the expected answer.
5. WRONG means a reliable answer was present but did not match the expected answer.
6. NO_ANSWER means the generated text ended before a reliable final answer could be identified.
7. COMPLIANT means the model followed the requested final-number or one-word output format.
8. NON_COMPLIANT does not necessarily mean the underlying answer was wrong.
9. Answer accuracy is CORRECT / all 10 exact-answer prompts.
10. Answered accuracy excludes NO_ANSWER and therefore must be reported separately from overall answer accuracy.
11. These exact-answer metrics should be interpreted together with the broader repetition and open-ended generation findings from Experiment 016.
12. Tokens/sec from Experiment 016D must not be interpreted as packed ternary acceleration because the ternary matrices were reconstructed into FP16 tensors.
