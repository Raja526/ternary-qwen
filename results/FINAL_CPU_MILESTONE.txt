===============================================================================
9. CLAIM BOUNDARIES
===============================================================================

The completed CPU work supports the following statements:

    - Selected ternary weights have a persistent packed representation.

    - The selected packed weights require 2,367,488 bytes versus
      17,825,792 FP16-equivalent bytes.

    - This corresponds to 7.529412x selected-weight storage compression.

    - OPT008 uses explicit AVX2 operations for packed-code decoding.

    - OPT008 is bit-exact to the frozen OPT006 packed-arithmetic reference
      in the performed operator, full-model, and multi-prompt validations.

    - Under the specified CPU benchmark protocol, OPT008 showed lower
      observed mean latency than OPT006 in both the original OPT008 benchmark
      and the final validation benchmark.


The completed CPU work does NOT establish:

    - universal CPU speedup

    - speedup on CPUs other than the tested system

    - speedup for arbitrary prompts or workloads

    - whole-model 7.529412x compression

    - general peak-memory reduction

    - GPU or CUDA acceleration

    - AVX-512 acceleration

    - FMA-based acceleration

    - full ternary dot-product vectorization

    - universal superiority of grain size 1

    - universal inferiority of output-row blocking
