CORRECTNESS BASIS
-----------------
GPU005 relies on the already-frozen GPU003/GPU004 correctness evidence.

GPU003 established isolated packed CUDA arithmetic agreement with the
frozen OPT006 packed arithmetic.

GPU004 established successful full-model integration of the same three
packed matrices.

GPU005 adds performance characterization; it does not replace those
correctness experiments.

ALLOWED CLAIMS
--------------
1. GPU005 executed an ordinary FP16 CUDA baseline and the frozen GPU004
   packed CUDA runtime under the documented local protocol.

2. The candidate retained the same three frozen packed matrices.

3. Both paths produced finite outputs.

4. The measured latency difference may be reported only as a
   protocol-specific observed result.

5. Selected-weight packed storage may be reported separately from
   whole-model GPU memory.

FORBIDDEN CLAIMS
----------------
GPU005 does NOT establish:
- universal GPU acceleration,
- isolated custom-kernel speedup,
- whole-model compression,
- general VRAM reduction,
- bit-exactness against the ordinary FP16 model,
- broad conclusions across GPU architectures,
- performance on modern datacenter GPUs.

The Quadro P1000 is the only GPU characterized here.

NEGATIVE RESULTS
----------------
A slower packed candidate is a valid experimental result.

GPU005 is considered complete when the benchmark executes correctly,
all mandatory measurements are present, the candidate uses exactly
the three frozen matrices, and the report accurately preserves the
observed result.
