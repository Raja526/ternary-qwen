FINAL EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED CONCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1. Parameter coverage alone is not a sufficient predictor of functional preservation.
E2. Matrix sensitivity is location- and interaction-dependent.
E3. Individual or pairwise compatibility does not guarantee compatibility in larger combinations.
E4. Aggregate accuracy preservation does not imply prediction-level behavioral preservation.
E5. Functional sensitivity is benchmark dependent.
E6. The current experiments demonstrate numerical ternarization behavior, not deployment-level compression or inference acceleration.


LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Results are specific to Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B and the tested matrices.
2. The frozen quantizer uses optimized-scale groupwise ternarization, group size 128, and threshold multiplier 0.5.
3. ARC-Challenge uses 299 validation questions.
4. HellaSwag Exp025 uses the deterministic first 2,000 validation examples rather than all 10,042 validation examples.
5. HellaSwag conclusions apply under the frozen length-normalized conditional continuation log-likelihood protocol used in Exp025.
6. ARC and HellaSwag use different frozen evaluation protocols.
7. McNemar p >= 0.05 does not establish statistical equivalence or prove preservation.
8. Positive accuracy deltas do not establish that ternarization improves the model.
9. The pairwise accuracy-interaction metric is a descriptive deviation from additive accuracy deltas, not a formal statistical interaction.
10. Correlation analyses combine overlapping and heterogeneous configurations and therefore should be interpreted descriptively.
11. No universal safe ternarization coverage threshold was established.
12. Ternary values are reconstructed into FP16; packed ternary storage, custom kernels, actual VRAM compression, and inference acceleration were not evaluated.
