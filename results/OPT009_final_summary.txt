Interpretation
--------------
All tested output-row blocking configurations preserved exact model behavior.

Under the frozen six-thread, 22-token CPU benchmark protocol, however,
none of the tested blocking configurations reduced observed mean latency
relative to OPT008.

Observed mean-latency changes:

    block 4:  +4.278%
    block 8:  +3.432%
    block 16: +2.288%

The small RSS differences do not establish a meaningful memory advantage.

No claim is made that these block sizes are universally suboptimal.
The result applies to the tested implementation, hardware, model,
selected matrices, and benchmark protocol.


Decision
--------
Output-row blocking is NOT retained.

Frozen OPT008 remains the CPU implementation carried forward to OPT010.

The OPT009 block-size investigation stops at:
    4
    8
    16

No broader block-size sweep is performed.

OPT009 is therefore recorded as a negative optimization result with
successful bit-exact correctness preservation.
